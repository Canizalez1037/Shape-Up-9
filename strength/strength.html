Strength Training

Strength training builds muscle, improves endurance, and helps keep your bones strong. You can work out with free weights, weight machines, resistance bands, or your own body weight.

Benefits of strength training

A regular strength-training program can help you:

  • Increase muscular strength and endurance
  • Maintain healthy bones and joints
  • Improve balance, posture, and everyday movement
  • Support a healthy metabolism

Getting started

Start with a routine that fits your current fitness level. Build good technique before adding heavier weights.

  1. Warm up with five to ten minutes of easy movement.
  2. Choose exercises for your legs, back, chest, arms, and core.
  3. Complete each movement slowly and with control.
  4. Allow your muscles time to recover between workouts.

Sample exercises

  • Squats: strengthen the legs and hips.
  • Push-ups: work the chest, shoulders, and arms.
  • Rows: build the upper back.
  • Planks: strengthen the core.
